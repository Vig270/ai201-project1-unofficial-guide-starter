PROGRAM: Undergraduate Computer Science & Engineering Overview (Watson College – Binghamton University)

PROGRAM OVERVIEW:
The Computer Science and Engineering programs at Watson College prepare students for careers in rapidly growing, high-demand technical fields including software engineering, systems, networking, and applied computing.

Graduates are prepared for careers in industry, government, or advanced academic study, and often enter well-compensated technical roles.

UNDERGRADUATE STRUCTURE:

First-Year Experience:
- Students begin in a common Engineering Design Division curriculum
- Exposure to multiple engineering fields before declaring a major
- Hands-on projects involving design and prototyping (e.g., Arduino-based systems, wearable tech, simple embedded systems)
- Early emphasis on practical engineering and computing applications

Major Declaration:
- At the end of the first year, students choose a specific engineering or computing major
- Options include:
  - Computer Science
  - Biomedical Engineering
  - Electrical and Computer Engineering
  - Mechanical Engineering
  - Systems Science and Industrial Engineering

COMPUTER SCIENCE PROGRAM FEATURES:
- Students begin CS coursework in their first semester
- Areas of specialization include:
  - Software systems
  - Operating systems
  - Networking
  - Web-based systems
  - Graphics and game development
  - Robotics and embedded systems
  - Microprocessor-based design

INTERDISCIPLINARY FLEXIBILITY:
- Students may pursue minors in:
  - Business
  - Mathematics
  - Arts and humanities
  - Music
  - Languages
  - Other sciences

LEARNING ENVIRONMENT:
- Emphasis on hands-on learning and applied problem solving
- Access to modern computing and engineering facilities
- State-of-the-art labs and equipment supporting experiential learning

CAREER PREPARATION:
- Strong focus on internships in industry and government
- Students gain real-world experience before graduation
- Internship experience often leads to full-time job offers
- Graduates enter fields such as software development, systems engineering, and data-driven industries

ADMISSIONS & CAMPUS EXPERIENCE:
- No personal interview required for admission
- Campus visits and student-led tours are available
- Information sessions cover academics, housing, student life, and campus resources
